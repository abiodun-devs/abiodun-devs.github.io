Production

Tunnel Fire Quantitative Risk Assessment (QRA) System

Python · PyQt · FDS · Monte Carlo evacuation modelling

A full reimplementation of a legacy Visual Basic road-tunnel QRA tool as a modern Python application. The system generates fire-simulation inputs, runs tunnel fire and evacuation scenarios across fire positions, traffic conditions and ventilation states, computes evacuee exposure (FED) and fatality estimates, and produces societal-risk F–N curves and risk indices for tunnel safety assessment.

The legacy algorithms were recovered through binary analysis and ported faithfully, with every constant traceable to the original and validated against reference outputs so results remain tunnel-independent.

Reverse engineeringNumerical modelling F–N curvesDesktop GUI
Production

Train Performance Simulation (TPS) System

Python · Rail operations modelling

A train-performance simulator that models a train's movement along a route from track geometry, gradients, speed limits, traction and braking characteristics. It computes run times, speed profiles and energy use, and supports headway and operational planning studies for urban and mainline rail.
